Our mission

Why we exist.

We help everyday people become everyday athletes.

The idea

Black Iron opened in 2013 on a simple belief: everyday people deserve real coaching too. Not a leaderboard culture that treats a Tuesday workout like a tryout. Not a sea of machines and a keycard. A coach who knows your name, programming with a plan behind it, and good people on every side of you.

The record

Thirteen years later, that idea has changed more than 3,000 lives across Frisco, Prosper, The Colony, Little Elm, McKinney, Plano, Celina, and North Dallas. Beginners, comeback stories, longtime athletes — we meet you where you are and coach every rep from there.

The floor

We are not CrossFit, not a big-box gym, not a boutique studio. Every member starts with Foundations — a week of one-on-one coaching — and every class runs at three levels, so nobody compromises and nobody gets thrown into the deep end.

“Your best years aren’t behind you. You just haven’t been trained properly yet. We fix the belief, then we forge the body.”

Three values

What we
hold to.

01

The work matters more than the clock

Show up, do the work, and let the calendar do its job. We measure progress in months and years — not in how wrecked you feel by noon.

02

Forge the body, guard the mind

Training builds more than muscle. Discipline, resilience, a clearer head — the mental side is half the reason the good people here keep showing up.

03

Person first, athlete second

You’re a parent, a professional, a person with a full plate. The program bends around your life, and a coach pulls you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself.

Six beliefs

How we run the floor.

Programmed, not random

Cycled progressions, planned intensity, strength and conditioning balanced every week. Nothing on the whiteboard is an accident.

Three levels, one class

Same movement, same clock, different target. Beginners and 10-year athletes train side by side. The system flexes so the member doesn’t have to.

Longevity is the backbone

We train you to love this for decades — not to survive a six-week challenge.

The 23 hours matter more than the 1

Nutrition, sleep, daily habits. What happens between sessions decides most of your results, so our coaching doesn’t stop at the whiteboard.

Foundations is non-negotiable

Every member starts with a week of one-on-one coaching. Basics, baseline, body. It’s why this works.

An ecosystem, not strangers

Coaches who check in, members who notice when you miss a week, and a community that makes showing up the easy part.

Veteran-owned

Built by a Marine.

Mike Manning is a medically retired Marine Corps combat veteran. He led a signals intelligence support team in Afghanistan, rebuilt himself as an athlete after his injuries, and came to Black Iron in 2014 for the thing he missed most from the Corps — a team that pushes each other to show up and be better. He bought out his partners in 2018 and has been sole owner since.

That’s why discipline, integrity, and service run through everything here. And it’s why military and first responders get 5% off any membership — no forms to hunt down, just tell us.
