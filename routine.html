How it works

What holds it together

Every class is sixty minutes and coached the whole way through. The strength and conditioning is planned in cycles weeks ahead, so the hard days and the easier days land on purpose instead of by mood. You don’t design anything and you don’t guess. You show up, a coach tells you what to do, and then watches you do it. Six class times a day Monday through Friday, plus Saturday at 9:30.

Programmed, not random

Cycled progressions, planned intensity, strength and conditioning balanced every week. That’s why month four still moves you forward instead of just making you tired.

Three levels, one floor

Every class runs at three levels. Same movement, same hour, different target. Somebody in their first month trains beside somebody in their tenth year and neither one gets shortchanged. No leaderboard.

Foundations before any class

Every member starts one-on-one with a coach. One week and three sessions, or two weeks and six sessions. You learn the movements and we set a baseline before a group is ever involved. You pay for it once and never again.

Before you book

What people ask before they start

I’ve quit gyms before. Why would this one hold?

Because the two things that were missing are the two things we do. Your training is programmed for you, so there’s nothing to figure out on a tired day. And a coach knows your name, so a missed week gets a text instead of silence. Most people don’t quit from lack of will. They quit from being on their own.

What does it cost?

Foundations is $360 for the one week, three session track, or $720 for the two week, six session track. That’s one time, never again. Membership is $200 a month for three classes a week, or $240 a month unlimited, which includes open gym seven days. Military, first responders and teachers get 5% off.

I haven’t trained in years. Is that a problem?

No, and it’s the most common thing we hear. Foundations exists for exactly this. One-on-one with a coach, no class to keep up with, nobody watching. Members are 16 and up and they’re everyday people with jobs.

How much time does this actually take?

Sixty minutes, door to door. Six coached class times a day Monday through Friday, plus Saturday at 9:30. Pick a time you can make on a busy week, not just a good one.

What if I have to stop?

Memberships start with 90 days, because real change takes longer than a month and we’d rather say that up front. After that it’s month to month, cancel with 30 days notice.
