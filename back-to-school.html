A note from Mike

The first day of school, I did both drop-offs by myself.

My wife teaches elementary school, so that morning she had to be at her own building early, getting her room ready to meet her class. So the run was mine. Our two-year-old to pre-K, then our five-year-old to his first day of kindergarten. I got a taste of what her every other morning looks like, and I have a new respect for it.

Then I drove home and the house was quiet. Not gone-for-an-hour quiet. The real kind. It had not sounded like that since May.

Here is what I have watched happen for thirteen years. That quiet fills itself. Errands, email, the thing you have been meaning to get to. Come November you are wondering where the fall went.

One hour of it could go to you. That is the whole pitch.

Mike Manning · Owner & Head Coach

The part people worry about

You don’t need to be
in shape first.

Every class runs at three levels. Same movement, same clock, different target. It’s why a first-month beginner can train next to a ten-year athlete without getting hurt or left behind.

Level 1 · Learn it

New, or coming back after years off. Own the movement before you load it. Nobody rushes you.

Level 2 · Build it

Solid mechanics, growing engine. Where most members live — and get strong for years.

Level 3 · Test it

For the seasoned athlete chasing an edge. Bigger loads, same standard, a coach watching anyway.

Before you book

The questions
parents ask.

I haven’t trained in years. Is that a problem?

No, and it’s the most common thing we hear. Foundations exists for exactly this — one-on-one with a coach, no class to keep up with, no crowd watching. In thirteen years, nobody has ever been too out of shape to start properly.

How long am I actually here?

Sixty minutes, door to door. The 9:30 class ends at 10:30. If you need to be somewhere at eleven, you will be.

What if my schedule changes mid-semester?

Six class times a day, Monday through Friday, plus a 9:30 on Saturday and open gym seven days a week. Most people move between times as the season changes. Nobody is locked to one hour.

Am I going to get hurt?

That’s the fear we take most seriously, because picking up your kids isn’t optional. We coach every rep, we scale to your level, and we pull you back when your ego is writing checks. Longevity is the point.

Can I try one class first?

Yes. A $30 day pass covers any class, and it’s credited toward your first month if you join. The consultation itself is free.

Is this CrossFit?

No. We ran as an affiliate for a decade and stepped away from the brand. What we do now is programmed strength and conditioning in planned cycles — no leaderboard culture, no treating a Tuesday workout like a tryout.

The first two weeks
are when it sticks.

A new routine sets in the window where everything else is already changing. That window is open right now, and it closes on its own by about October.
