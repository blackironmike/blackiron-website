How it works

How the training actually works here

Sixty minutes, with a coach on the floor the whole hour. Programmed in cycles and planned weeks ahead, never invented that morning. Every class is written three ways, so a beginner and a ten-year veteran train in the same room. Six times a day, Monday through Friday.

Everybody starts with Foundations

Before your first group class you train one-on-one with a coach. You learn the movements and we set your baseline. One week and three sessions, or two weeks and six. You pay once and never again.

Three levels, one floor
