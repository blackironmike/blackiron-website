How it works

How the hour is run

Classes start on time and run sixty minutes. A coach runs the whole hour, so you’re never standing there guessing what to do next. The strength and conditioning is programmed in cycles and planned weeks ahead, so the hard days and the easier days land on purpose. We coach every rep. We push when you’re ready and we pull you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself.

Six class times a day

Coached classes six times a day Monday through Friday, plus Saturday at 9:30. Pick the one that fits inside your school day and keep it. Most members train three days a week.

Foundations first

Before any group class you train one-on-one with a coach. One week and three sessions, or two weeks and six sessions. You learn the movements and we set your baseline, so your first class isn’t the first time you’ve done any of it. You pay for it once and never again.

Three levels, one floor

Every class is written at three levels. On day one you do the version that fits the body you have, in the same hour and the same room as people who have been here for years.

Questions parents ask us

What times can I actually come?

Six coached class times a day Monday through Friday, plus Saturday at 9:30. Classes run sixty minutes. Pick the one that fits between drop-off and pick-up and keep it.

What does it cost?

Standard is $200 a month for three classes a week. Unlimited is $240 a month and includes open gym seven days. Everyone starts with Foundations first: $360 for the one week, three session track, or $720 for the two week, six session track. That’s one time, never again. Military, first responders and teachers get 5% off.

I haven’t trained in years. Am I too far behind?

No. You start one-on-one with a coach before you ever walk into a group class, and every class is written at three levels. Starting over is the normal case here, not the exception.

Am I locked into a contract?

Ninety days to start, because that’s honestly how long it takes to feel the change. After that you’re month to month with 30 days notice.

What if I’m worried about getting hurt?

That’s what we pay the most attention to. Foundations comes first, the program is planned instead of made up that morning, and a coach watches every rep. Person first, athlete second. Members are 16 and up.
