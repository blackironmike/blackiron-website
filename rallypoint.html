Why we’re doing it

Rally Point is eight weeks of training together through the holidays. No testing. No benchmarks. Just showing up, having fun and doing it as a team.

The bigger reason is belonging. Being a member here means you’re part of something larger than your own workout. For the next eight weeks, your team is counting on you to walk through the door.

It also carries us into the new gym in January, together.

How it works

  1. Sign up. You land on one of six teams, each led by a coach.
  2. Show up. Every class earns points for your team. Your check-in counts on its own.
  3. Do more. Bring a guest. Drop off a toy. Stay active on the road. Take on the weekly challenge.
  4. Watch the board. Team and individual leaderboards, in the app and on the gym screen.

Prizes along the way.

Six teams, six coaches

You find out which team is yours the moment you sign up. Your coach is your captain for all eight weeks.
