What we use
and pay for ourselves.

This is a shelf, not a store. Nothing on this page is bought from us. It is the stuff that lives in the gym, that we use ourselves, and that we hand people when they ask us what to get. Some of the links pay us a commission. That is said out loud everywhere it applies, and it has never once decided what goes on the page.

How this works. Some of the links below are tracked, and we earn a commission when you buy through them. It costs you nothing extra, and the price is the same as going direct. We only list what we stock, use, or would recommend to a member standing in front of us. Nothing gets on this page because it pays better.

Supplements.
The whole shelf.

We are a Thorne practitioner partner, so everything we carry is NSF Certified for Sport: independently tested for purity, potency and banned substances. That matters whether you compete or you just want to know what is actually in the tub.

Commission. The supplement links are tracked to our Thorne dispensary and we earn on anything bought through them. It costs you nothing extra.

The year-round foundation is four products: Whey Protein Isolate, Multi-Vitamin Elite, Amino Complex and Creatine. Two more move depending on whether you are in a cut or a build. The full shelf, the pre-built stacks and the reasoning behind each one live on the supplements page.

How something
gets on this page.

Three rules, and they are the reason this page is short.

01

We actually use it

We own it, stock it, or have put real hours on it. Nothing gets recommended off a spec sheet.

02

It survives the no-commission test

We would tell a member to buy it with nothing in it for us. If that is not true, it does not go up.

03

It comes off when we stop

If it leaves the floor, it leaves the page. A list like this is only worth reading if it can shrink.

That is also why there is no barbell section yet, no shoes, no recovery hardware. Those are coming as we finish testing them and sort out who we actually want to send you to. We would rather have one honest category than six padded ones.

Training somewhere else and want a straight answer on what to buy? Ask us in the gym, or send a message. We will tell you the same thing either way, including when the cheap option is fine.

The gear matters
less than the coaching.

All of this is downstream of showing up and being coached properly. If you want the part that actually moves the needle, start there.
