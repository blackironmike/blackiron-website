This is a shelf, not a store. Nothing on this page is bought from us. It is the stuff that lives in the gym, that we use ourselves, and that we hand people when they ask us what to get. Some of the links pay us a commission. That is said out loud everywhere it applies, and it has never once decided what goes on the page.

How this works. Some of the links below are tracked, and we earn a commission when you buy through them. It costs you nothing extra. You pay Thorne’s price, not ours, and we never mark anything up. How something
gets on this page.

Three rules, and they are the reason this page is short.

01

We actually use it

We own it, stock it, or have put real hours on it. Nothing gets recommended off a spec sheet.

02

It survives the no-commission test

We would tell a member to buy it with nothing in it for us. If that is not true, it does not go up.

03

It comes off when we stop

If it leaves the floor, it leaves the page. A list like this is only worth reading if it can shrink.

That is why two of the three shelves above are empty. We would rather have one honest category than six padded ones, and an empty shelf that tells you why is worth more than a full one that cannot.

The gear matters
less than the coaching.
