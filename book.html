Questions

Before you send it.

Do I need to be in shape first?

No. Every member starts with Foundations, a week of one-on-one coaching to learn the basics and set a baseline. Every class runs three levels, so you start where you are.

Is this CrossFit?

No. No leaderboard, no random workouts. We program strength and conditioning in planned cycles, and a coach runs every class.

What does it cost?

It depends on what fits you: group classes, personal training, or a mix. Your coach will walk you through the options when they reach out, so you’re comparing the right things.
