Honest answers

The questions people actually ask.

I’m not in shape enough to start.

That’s exactly what Foundations is for. You get a full week of one-on-one coaching to learn the basics and set a baseline before you ever join a class. Then Level 1 meets you where you are — not where a leaderboard thinks you should be.

Will I get hurt?

Our first job is keeping you training for decades, not chasing one heroic Tuesday. Every rep is coached, every workout has three levels, and we’ll pull you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself. Soreness, yes. Recklessness, no.

What does it cost?

Standard is $200 a month, Unlimited with open gym is $240, open gym only is $50, and personal training runs $120–150 an hour. Prices are on this page because we don’t do games. Want to try first? A $30 day pass covers any class, credited toward your first month if you join.

I bounced off CrossFit and big gyms before.

So did a lot of our members. There’s no leaderboard culture here — three levels on one floor means nobody’s comparing your bar to anyone else’s. Person first, athlete second. Come meet the good people and feel the difference.
