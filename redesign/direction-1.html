04

How class works

Three levels, one floor.

Every workout is programmed three ways before you walk in. A first-timer and a ten-year athlete train side by side — same hour, same coach, no compromise.

1

Level one Build

Own the movement first. Lighter loads, tighter positions, a base that lasts. Where everyone starts after Foundations.

2

Level two Load

Add weight and pace without losing form. Where most of the floor lives, most of the year.

3

Level three Push

Heavier bars and a faster engine, for athletes with years under the bar and something to chase.

The system flexes so the member doesn’t have to.

Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

“I’m not in shape enough to start.”

You’re exactly who Foundations is for. A full week of one-on-one coaching before you ever step into a class — we set your baseline, teach the basics, and build from wherever you actually are. Nobody gets thrown into the deep end here.

Will I get hurt?

Our whole system is built against it. Every class runs at three levels, every rep gets coached, and we pull you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself. Longevity is the backbone of the programming — we want you training for decades, not weeks.

What does it cost?

Memberships are custom to your goals, so we’d rather talk than quote a number that might not fit you. The consult is free with zero pressure, and a $30 day pass lets you try the floor first — credited toward your first month if you join.

I bounced off CrossFit and big-box gyms before.

So did a lot of our members. There’s no leaderboard culture here — three levels in every class, a coach on the floor for every rep, and a rule we take seriously: person first, athlete second.
