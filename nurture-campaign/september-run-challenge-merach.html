MILES IN AT HOME.

Hey Black Iron Family,

We are two weeks into the run challenge and a lot of you are putting real mileage on the board. Walking, jogging, running, all of it counts, as long as you went and got it on purpose.

Here is the question I have gotten more than any other since we started: what do you use at home when you are not at the gym, or when it is a hundred degrees outside and going for a run sounds miserable?

A couple of you have already asked and gone out and bought something, so let me give everybody the same straight answer.
What is in my garage
 

A MERACH T12 Pro treadmill. I paid for it myself. Well over a hundred miles on it now, most of them slow incline walks in the morning while I answer emails after the wife and kids head out.

One problem in all that time. The belt stretched in the Texas garage heat and started skipping. Two tension screws on the back, about ninety seconds, fixed. That is the entire complaint list.

Finding an hour is easier than people think, too. Walk at two or three miles an hour and you have covered two or three miles without leaving the house. Do it while you take the call you do not have to talk on.

Two things before you click. The T12 I actually own is not in the sale. The walking pad that is on sale, I have never stood on. So what I can tell you is that this company built something that has held up to my mileage and my Texas garage, and that is exactly where my recommendation stops.
