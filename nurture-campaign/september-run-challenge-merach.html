Getting miles at home
 

The question I have gotten more than any other since we started: what do you use at home when you cannot get to the gym, or when it is a hundred degrees out and a run sounds miserable?

I have a MERACH T12 Pro treadmill in the garage and a walking pad under my standing desk. Between the two of them I get most of my mileage without ever leaving the house. Slow incline walks in the morning while I answer emails, and the pad going while I work.
Mine, in the garage. Most of those miles happened right there.

The treadmill has well over a hundred miles on it now. One problem in all that time. The belt stretched in the Texas garage heat and started skipping, which two tension screws and about ninety seconds fixed. That is the entire complaint list.

The math is friendlier than people expect, too. Walk at two or three miles an hour and an hour gets you two or three miles. Do it while you take the call you do not have to talk on.
Breakfast, email and a couple of miles at the same time. The screen folds flat into a desk.
