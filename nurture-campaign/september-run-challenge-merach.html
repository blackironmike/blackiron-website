One rule, and it is the whole point: the miles have to be intentional. Going out for a walk counts. Laps around the office do not. We are not counting the steps you were going to take anyway. We are counting the ones you chose.

Which brings me to the question I have been asked more than any other this month.

 

“What treadmill should I get for the house?”

I own a MERACH T12 Pro. I paid for it with my own money, it lives in my garage after a stint under my desk, and I have put well over a hundred miles on it. Most of those miles are slow incline walks, which have treated my body a lot better than beating myself up ever did.

One real problem in all that time. The belt stretched in the Texas garage heat and started skipping. Two tension screws on the back, ninety seconds, fixed. Finding the wrench took longer than the repair. That is the entire complaint list.

MERACH is running a sale from September 15 through 30, up to 40 percent off. Two things you should know before you click anything.

The T12, the one I actually own, is not in the sale. The walking pad that is on sale, the UltraWalk W60 Plus at $299.99 during the sale, is one I have never stood on.
