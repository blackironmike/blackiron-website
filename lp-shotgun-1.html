How it works

How the twenty weeks actually get used

You start with Foundations, one-on-one with a coach, before you ever step into a group class. After that you’re in classes with everybody else. Sixty minutes, coached start to finish, programmed in cycles and planned weeks ahead instead of made up that morning. Six class times a day Monday through Friday, plus Saturday at 9:30, so three sessions fit in a normal week without rearranging your life.

Foundations comes first

Every member starts one-on-one, before any group class. You learn the movements, we set a baseline, and you understand what your body is doing. Nobody gets dropped into a class and told to keep up.

Three levels, one floor

Every class is written at three levels. Week one and year six train the same hour with the same coach, just with different weight on the bar. You’re never the only one who can’t do it.

We coach every rep

A coach is watching. We push when you need it and we pull you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself. That’s how you get twenty weeks in a row instead of two good weeks and a tweaked back.

After that it’s $200 a month for three classes a week, or $240 a month unlimited, which includes open gym seven days. Military, first responders and teachers get 5% off.

There’s a 90 day initial commitment, then month to month with 30 days notice. Ninety days is what it takes to know whether this works for you. It’s also short enough to walk away from.
