What we offer

Two ways to train

Every hour on the board is one of these.

Strength & Conditioning

The flagship. A coached 60-minute class that blends functional strength, conditioning, and skill work — programmed in cycles, not random. Every class runs at three levels, so beginners and advanced athletes train side by side without compromise.

New here? Every member starts with a free consultation and the Foundations week — one-on-one coaching before your first class. See how getting started works.

Open Gym

Self-directed training, seven days a week, between classes and after hours. Key fob entry, a climate-controlled floor, and access to all of our programming — follow the plan or run your own.

The barbell and Olympic lifts live here year-round, and official HYROX affiliate programming runs in open gym seven days a week. More on open gym.
