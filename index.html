Every class is programmed at three levels. Same movement pattern, same clock, different target — so a first-month beginner and a 10-year athlete train side by side without either one compromising. The system flexes so the member doesn’t have to.

FAQ

Asked every week.

I’m not in shape enough to start.

That’s exactly what Foundations is for. Your first week is one-on-one with a coach — no class to keep up with, no crowd. We meet you at your baseline and build from there. In 13 years, nobody has ever been too out of shape to start properly.

Will I get hurt?

Our whole system is built around not getting you hurt. A coach on every rep. Three levels, so you’re never pushed past what you’re ready for. Programming that plans intensity instead of chasing exhaustion. And when you’re getting ahead of yourself, we pull you back — that’s part of the job.

What does it cost?

Standard is $200 a month, Unlimited with open gym is $240, open gym only is $50, and personal training runs $120–150 an hour. Prices are in writing because we don’t do games. Want to try first? A $30 day pass covers any class, credited toward your first month if you join.

I bounced off CrossFit and big gyms before.

So did a lot of our members. There’s no leaderboard culture here — three levels in every class means Tuesday isn’t a tryout. Person first, athlete second. You’ll work hard, and nobody will make it weird.
