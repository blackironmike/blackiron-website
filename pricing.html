The comparison

About that
$10 gym.

A $10 membership is priced for people who stop coming. The model only works if most members never show up. Ours is the opposite — we only work when you show up, so everything in the price exists to keep you showing up.

What $10 a month buys

  • A door code and a room of machines.
  • A workout you have to invent yourself.
  • Nobody who knows your name.
  • No plan for month two.

What your membership buys

  • A coach on every rep, in every class.
  • Programming in planned cycles — strength and conditioning balanced every week.
  • Three levels in one class, so you’re never in over your head.
  • A monthly body-comp scan with every class membership, so results stay honest.
  • Good people who notice when you show up — and when you don’t.

If all you need is a room, a big-box is honestly cheaper — take it. If you want coaching, a plan, and people, that’s what the price is for.

Where every membership starts

First,
Foundations.

Before you pick a plan, you start like every member before you: a free consultation, then the Foundations week — one-on-one coaching to learn the basics, set a baseline, and understand your body. It’s non-negotiable, and it’s the reason a first-month beginner can train next to a 10-year athlete without getting hurt or left behind.

How your first week works

01

Book a free consult

Standard, Unlimited, open gym, or personal training. By the end of your Foundations week you’ll know which one fits — and so will your coach.

Fair questions

Money questions,
straight answers.

Why does it cost more than a big-box gym?

Because you’re buying coaching, not access. A coach on every rep, programming in planned cycles, three levels in every class, a monthly body-comp scan — that’s payroll, planning, and attention, not a door code. If a room of machines keeps you training, we’re genuinely glad. For most people we meet, it didn’t.

Which membership should I pick?

Train three days a week — Standard. Want unlimited classes plus the open floor — Unlimited. Run your own program — Open Gym. Want one coach and one plan — personal training. The free consult sorts it out in one conversation, and the Foundations week confirms it.

Can I try it before I join?

Yes. A $30 day pass covers any class, and it’s credited toward your first month if you join. The consultation itself is free.

How does canceling work?

Memberships start with a 90-day commitment — change takes time, and we’d rather have people who are giving it a real shot. After that it’s month to month: give us 30 days’ notice and you’re done. No retention scripts, no hoops.

Do you offer discounts?

Military and first responders take 5% off any membership. We’re veteran-owned — it matters here.

When do classes run?

Morning through evening — see this week’s schedule. Open gym is available 7 days a week, between classes and after hours.
