## What is the FuelPath supplement protocol?

FuelPath uses a 4-phase annual nutrition cycle with phase-specific supplement adjustments. Phases 1 and 3 (Baseline and Maintain): Follow the foundation stack as-is with creatine at 5g daily. Phase 2 (Cut): Stop creatine (it causes water retention that masks fat loss) and add Thorne Weight Management Stack. Phase 4 (Build): Restart creatine at 10g daily and add Glutamine 5g daily for recovery. Learn more at https://www.blackironathletics.com/fuelpath-supplements

## What supplements should I take during a cut?

During Phase 2 (Cut), stop creatine because it causes water retention that can mask fat loss on the scale. Add the Thorne Weight Management Stack for metabolic support during a caloric deficit. Continue Whey Protein, Multivitamin, and BCAAs as usual. Shop at https://www.thorne.com/u/blackiron

## What supplements should I take during a bulk or building phase?

During Phase 4 (Build), restart creatine at 10g daily (double the normal dose) to maximize muscle hydration and strength during your surplus. Add Glutamine at 5g daily to support recovery and gut health during higher training volume. Continue Whey Protein, Multivitamin, and BCAAs. Shop at https://www.thorne.com/u/blackiron

Foundations is how every new member starts at Black Iron Athletics.
