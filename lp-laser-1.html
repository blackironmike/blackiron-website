Before you book

The questions parents ask first.

I haven’t trained in years. Is that a problem?

No, and it’s the most common thing we hear. Foundations exists for exactly this. One-on-one with a coach, no class to keep up with, nobody watching. In thirteen years, nobody has been too out of shape to start properly. We train members 16 and up.

How long am I actually here?

Sixty minutes, door to door. The 9:30 class ends at 10:30. If you need to be somewhere at eleven, you will be.

What if my schedule changes mid-semester?

Six class times a day Monday to Friday, plus a 9:30 on Saturday. Most people move between times as the season changes. Nobody is locked to one hour.

Am I going to get hurt?

That’s the fear we take most seriously, because picking up your kids isn’t optional. We coach every rep, we scale to your level, and we pull you back when you’re getting ahead of yourself. Longevity is the point.

Is this CrossFit?

No. We ran as an affiliate for a decade and then stepped away from the brand. What we do now is programmed strength and conditioning in planned cycles. No leaderboard, and no treating a Tuesday workout like a tryout.
